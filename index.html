Library Management System

Whether you are a student, a librarian or the head of an organisation — LibraryOS gives you one calm, fast place to find books, track every loan and see your whole library at a glance.

One system for every shelf, every member and every return.

LibraryOS replaces paper registers and scattered spreadsheets. Staff catalogue books, register students, issue and receive items and watch overdue and lost books — while administrators follow live reports across the whole organisation.

About LibraryOS

Built to make libraries feel effortless — for the people who run them and the people who use them.

Libraries hold the most valuable thing a community has: knowledge. LibraryOS keeps that knowledge organised, safe and easy to reach. It is a web-based system that works on any phone, tablet or computer — nothing to install.

Every organisation gets its own secure space, so a school, a college or a community library can manage its own books, members and staff independently.

  • Works on smartphones, tablets and laptops
  • Light and dark mode for comfortable reading, day or night
  • Separate, secure space for every registered organisation
  • Powered by Billan Company — made to grow with you
